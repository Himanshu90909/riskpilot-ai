## Selected Approach: Editorial Trust Layer

### Design Movement
Swiss International Typographic Style translated into a contemporary enterprise fintech product: disciplined hierarchy, editorial spacing, modular information blocks, and precise alignment softened with modern product polish.

### Core Principles
1. **Trust through clarity:** every risk signal, decision, and status should be readable at a glance.
2. **Operational calm:** use restraint, generous whitespace, and controlled contrast so urgent moments stand out.
3. **Evidence before decoration:** charts, timelines, and data relationships are the visual center of gravity.
4. **Human accountability:** AI recommendations are prominent, but overrides, reasons, and audit trails are first-class interactions.

### Color Philosophy
Use an ink-black sidebar and charcoal text to signal seriousness, a warm paper background to avoid sterile dashboard fatigue, and signal-teal as the ownable accent for positive action, system health, and AI activity. Reserve amber, coral, and rose for risk semantics only; never use them as general decoration. Surfaces should have subtle cool-gray depth rather than loud gradients.

### Layout Paradigm
A persistent asymmetric operations rail anchors the experience. The main canvas uses editorial split sections: KPI strip, wide evidence chart, narrow watchlist, and action-oriented lower modules. Detail views should feel like a case file with a strong left identity column and a right evidence stack rather than a centered card wall.

### Signature Elements
- A small **signal rail** motif: vertical teal bars and micro pulse marks beside active AI or system states.
- **Case-file labels**: uppercase eyebrow labels with compact tracking, thin rules, and timestamp metadata.
- A **risk ring / score marker** that uses a precise arc and a small state dot rather than a generic progress bar.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like an operator's console: direct, reversible, and informative. Clicking a transaction opens a focused investigation drawer, toggling a rule immediately updates the engine preview, and running a simulation surfaces a clear decision with rationale. Use toast confirmations for completed actions and never hide the reason behind a hover-only affordance.

### Animation
Keep motion purposeful and under 280ms. Let dashboard modules enter with a small opacity/translate reveal, staggered by 40ms; let the signal rail pulse only while an investigation is running; use snappy scale feedback on buttons; animate the risk ring sweep when a score is calculated. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep data tables instant.

### Typography System
- **Display / navigation:** Space Grotesk, 600–700, used for product name, page titles, KPI values, and primary actions.
- **Body / data:** IBM Plex Sans, 400–600, used for descriptions, labels, table data, and helper copy.
- **Monospace:** IBM Plex Mono, 500, used for transaction IDs, API snippets, timestamps, and model versions.
- Hierarchy: eyebrow 10–11px uppercase with tracking; body 13–14px; titles 24–32px; KPI figures 26–34px. Avoid oversized marketing typography inside the dashboard.

### Brand Essence
**RiskPilot AI is the autonomous risk layer for payment teams that need to investigate faster, explain every decision, and protect revenue without adding operational drag.**

Personality: **measured, incisive, accountable**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise and evidence-led. CTAs are verbs that communicate an operational outcome. Microcopy should explain what the system did and what the operator can do next, never rely on vague AI hype.

Example lines:
- “See the signals behind every decision.”
- “Run the attack. Measure the exposure. Tune the response.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact **RP monogram inside a split shield**: two offset vertical planes form a protected aperture, with a small teal signal notch at the center. The mark should work as a symbol at small sizes; pair it with a custom-spaced “RiskPilot AI” wordmark in Space Grotesk rather than relying on default browser text styling.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Teal — #19C6B1.** It is distinct from conventional fintech blue, reads clearly on ink and paper, and communicates a living risk signal instead of a static corporate accent.

## Style Decisions
- Keep dashboard surfaces warm and light; use deep ink only for navigation, hero moments, and high-importance case headers.
- Use risk colors semantically: teal for trusted/approved, amber for review, coral for high risk, rose-red for critical/block.
- Avoid centered page compositions, purple gradients, excessive rounded cards, and generic “AI magic” language.
- Treat all metrics as clearly labeled synthetic demo data for NovaPay.
- Surfaces should read as editorial case-file modules with thin rules, disciplined columns, and restrained depth; avoid pillowy rounded SaaS cards as the dominant layout language.
- Product imagery must be evidence-first: prioritize risk rings, transaction traces, timelines, audit metadata, and decision rationale over abstract AI/radar illustrations.
- The signal rail is a required brand motif: use Signal Teal #19C6B1 as narrow vertical bars, pulse dots, or active-state notches beside AI activity and system-state moments across every route.
