# Official Razorpay Buildathon Notes

Source: https://razorpay.com/buildathon/

Verified on 2026-08-22. The page describes a student-only AI Builder Internship program with no resume screening, aptitude test, or group discussion. Applicants choose a track, build something real, show a public repository, a five-minute pitch video, and the architecture; if the work has signal, shortlisted builders go to a panel.

For the **AI Risk Manager** track, the stated bar is a working detector, verifier, or auto-responder for one class of loss, with measured precision and recall on a held-out test set. The page also asks for honest metrics including false-positive cost and a strictly defense-only implementation. It says the offer is ₹75,000 monthly stipend for 6 or 12 months, in-person in Bangalore from September, for shortlisted builders.

This note is context for the RiskPilot interview demo. Project metrics are synthetic and should be labeled as demo/evaluation evidence, not production claims.
